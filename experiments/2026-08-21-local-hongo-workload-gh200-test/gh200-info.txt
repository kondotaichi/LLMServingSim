9. Recommended simulator parameters
-----------------------------------
Use two levels depending on what the simulator represents.

A. Physical / RDMA network model
- nominal_link_bw: 25.0 GB/s
- measured_host_rdma_bw: 24.79 GB/s
- measured_gpu_rdma_bw: 24.76 GB/s
- link_latency_baseline: approximately 1.45 us

Recommended if the simulator models the network/fabric separately from NCCL:
- link_bw = approximately 24.8 GB/s
- link_latency = approximately 1.45 us

B. Application-facing NCCL GPU-to-GPU model
- large-message NCCL P2P bandwidth: approximately 22.96 GB/s
- small-message NCCL sendrecv latency: approximately 16.8 us

Recommended if the simulator models end-to-end GPU communication:
- effective_link_bw = approximately 23.0 GB/s
- effective_link_latency = approximately 16-17 us

For collectives:
- Do not derive collective performance solely from P2P bandwidth.
- Prefer the measured 8-node NCCL curves.
- 8-node AllGather and AllToAll reach approximately 24-25 GB/s bus bandwidth
  for large messages.
- Use the measured AllReduce curve directly when modeling TP-style workloads.
